Privacy Policy

Fedora Rclone Sync is a personal OAuth application used by its owner with rclone to access and synchronize files stored in Google Drive.

Information collected

This application does not independently collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Google Drive access

The application requests access to Google Drive so that rclone can synchronize files between Google Drive and the owner's local computer.

Files are accessed only for the purpose of the owner's personal file synchronization workflow.

Data sharing

No Google Drive files or personal information are sold or shared with third parties by this application.

Data storage

OAuth credentials used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's computer. This website does not store Google Drive files, OAuth credentials, or Google account information.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use by its owner.
